Before you start.

Is this an official WhatsApp product?

This is an independent Dumela extension for WhatsApp Web. It is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

What happens to my chats?

Only selected interface elements are hidden. Chats, messages and your account settings stay intact.

Does the extension collect my data?

No personal data or chat content is collected. Preferences are stored locally in your browser.
